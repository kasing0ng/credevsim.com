I am Kasing Ng, a California-licensed CPA and former Big 4 fund auditor, with a BS in Business Administration from UC Berkeley. I spent years auditing real estate funds and REITs, tracing every figure in the financials back to something you can point to. That is the habit I bring to a deal: a number you cannot support does not belong in front of an investor.

Today I work with small real estate sponsors across multifamily and commercial real estate on the one part of the deal most models skip: the after-tax number the sponsor and the LPs actually keep.

What I actually do

I read the after-tax side of a deal, independently. Specifically:

  • After-tax LP and GP returns, carried past the pre-tax IRR to what survives depreciation recapture, capital gains, and the 3.8% surtax.
  • Depreciation recapture at exit, split correctly between the capped §1250 rate and the ordinary-income §1245 rate, so a bonus-depreciation deal does not understate its exit tax.
  • Catch-up depreciation on a property held a few years without a cost-segregation study, recovered on the current-year return through a single §3115 / §481(a) filing, no amended returns. On an asset bought in 2020 or 2021, that catch-up is often the largest single tax number on the deal, and it lands on the sponsor's return.
  • The §469 passive-loss trap, 1031 exchanges, and Opportunity Zone treatment, since each one changes the after-tax answer.

Independent, and non-attest

This matters, so I state it plainly. I do not sell deals and I earn no commission. I do not sell cost-segregation studies, and I do not file your returns. I am an independent modeler and advisor to the sponsor. I hand you a traceable after-tax read that you can take to whoever prepares your taxes. The CPA credential is stated as background; the work I do here is non-attest.

The tool

Because I kept finding the same gaps, I built the model I wished those deals had come in on. It is called CREDevSim, a desktop app that runs on your own machine so nothing about your deal touches a server. It carries the deal to the after-tax LP number, splits recapture correctly, and lets every figure trace back to a formula you can open. It is free to use.

我是伍嘉昇，持有加州執照的美國註冊會計師，曾任四大會計師事務所的基金審計師，並擁有加州大學柏克萊分校 (UC Berkeley) 商業管理學士學位。我花了多年審計房地產基金與 REIT，把財報上的每一個數字都追溯到站得住腳的依據。這就是我帶進每一筆交易的習慣：一個無法支持的數字，不該出現在投資人面前。

現在我與中小型房地產發起人合作，涵蓋多戶住宅與商業地產，專注在大多數模型略過的一環：發起人與 LP 真正留在手上的稅後數字。

我實際做的事

我獨立解讀交易的稅後面向，具體包括：

  • 稅後 LP 與 GP 回報，從稅前 IRR 一路算到折舊回補、資本利得與 3.8% 附加稅之後真正剩下的部分。
  • 出場時的折舊回補，正確區分受上限的 §1250 稅率與一般所得的 §1245 稅率，使採用加速折舊的交易不會低估其出場稅負。
  • 持有數年、未做成本分離研究之資產的追補折舊，透過單次 §3115 / §481(a) 申報在當年度報稅表上一次認列，無需修改以往年度報稅表。對 2020 或 2021 年購入的資產而言，這筆追補往往是整筆交易中最大的單一稅務數字，且落在發起人的報稅表上。
  • §469 被動損失陷阱、1031 交換與機會區 (Opportunity Zone) 處理，因為每一項都會改變稅後的答案。

獨立，且屬非鑑證

這一點很重要，我直接說清楚。我不銷售交易，也不收取任何佣金。我不銷售成本分離研究，也不代為報稅。我是發起人的獨立模型建置者與顧問。我交給你一份可追溯的稅後解讀，你可以帶給任何替你報稅的人。CPA 資格僅作為背景說明；我在這裡所做的工作屬非鑑證性質。
