Why now

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act restored 100% bonus depreciation for property placed in service after January 19, 2025, with no phase-down. Paired with a cost segregation study, that can turn a large share of your building basis into a year-one write-off. If you bought or are buying right now, the window to capture this correctly is open, and the tax-basis and K-1 reporting around it is stricter than it has ever been.
